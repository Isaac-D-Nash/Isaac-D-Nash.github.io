Momentum Bound

Overview

Partway through my first year, some of the people on my course decided our games would share interwoven lore. Momentum Bound grew out of that, and it still gets updates today.

I'd wanted to make a roguelike for a while, since I always found The Binding of Isaac to be an extremely fun game, and I wanted to be able to look at something like it and think "I did that." The chance arrived when our course set us a 2D project, so I took it and started building Momentum Bound. Through our separate projects, a few of us on the course had coincidentally each made a character named Craig — we decided it was the same man, and Momentum Bound became the place that ties all of Phase 1's lore together. I still work on it now.

Design and lore

It's a roguelike inspired by The Binding of Isaac, bringing together most of the lore of "Craig" up to the point this game released.

To tie the lore in with my coursemates' games, I borrowed designs across the group — for example, Craig's human design from Team Chiburren's Everdew. I also folded in references to pop culture I like, plus more nods to my coursemates' games, and looked for ways to hide them so players could find them rather than have them handed over. Characters like Butters and Margarine from Everdew appear as bosses you'll have to play the game to meet. There's also an in-game case report that lets players pick up most of the story without needing to have played the other games first.

Where it's headed

If I keep going, I'll add more lore, more bosses, and a lot more polish to make sure the game looks and plays well.

My most recent fix repaired controller support that had broken, along with the lore-pages DLC, which had broken in a previous update — that one took a while, since I'd been busy with other things. For future updates I want to take a leaf out of Edmund McMillen's book and ship bigger DLC drops that add new floors, bosses, enemies, and items. I'll keep working on it for a while yet; it's a genuinely fun game to work on, and it's nice having something in the background to come back to when I get bored or want something familiar.
